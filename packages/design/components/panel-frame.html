Panel frame

The enclosure every other control-room component is mounted inside: a brushed steel frame with a visible fastener at each corner. Kid mode keeps the same four-fastener structure, just with softer shadows and a warmer, rounder frame.

Mixer line 2

A panel frame carries no meaning of its own — it is a plain <section> (or any container) with aria-labelledby pointing at a real heading, so a screen reader announces the panel by its name, not by the fact that it happens to look like steel.

Uses --cb-color-surface-panel, --cb-color-border, --cb-elevation-raised-2 and --cb-radius-lg. Try the Kid mode toggle above — the same markup, larger radius, softer shadow, warmer fill.
