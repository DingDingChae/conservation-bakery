Toast / status line

A brief, non-blocking status message. The role="status" region stays in the document at all times — only its data-visible attribute and text change — because a live region that is inserted fresh each time is announced inconsistently across screen readers.

Auto-dismisses after 6 seconds, or press the dismiss button. Its left edge uses --cb-color-text-amber as an accent, and the entrance animation is skipped entirely under reduced motion.
